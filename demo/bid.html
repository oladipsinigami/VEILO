Seal a bid

A sealed-bid auction has two halves. This is the bidder's half: name a price, lock collateral, and prove the price later. Until you reveal it, the chain holds a commitment — a fingerprint with no number attached.

Step 1 — seal

Loading the auction document…

The bid is what you are willing to pay, and it stays secret until you reveal it. The collateral is what you actually send to escrow now: it must be at least the bid, because the winner pays the clearing price out of it. Whatever is left comes back to the refund address. This page has no server and makes no network calls after it loads — the sealing happens in your browser.

Nothing sealed yet

Fill in the three fields above. You will get a payment request, a 512-byte memo, and a reveal ticket to save.

Step 2 — publish your reveal

After the auction closes, open the ticket this page downloaded and turn it back into the 40 bytes that open your commitment. Publishing is permissionless: anyone can post it, and the auditor matches it against the commitment already on chain. A bid nobody can open is not counted, even though the collateral was sent.

The sealing code is web/src/lib/bid.ts, bundled into bid/app.js and checked into this repository so the page runs from static files. The auditor that recomputes the clearing price is on the audit page.
