Veilo — independent auction audit

Recompute a sealed-bid auction's clearing price from chain ciphertexts, in your browser, using nothing but the seller's public viewing key. No server, no account, no trust in whoever ran the auction.

What this is and is not

The ciphertexts below are produced by Zcash's real Sapling note encryption (sapling_crypto) and carry real 512-byte Veilo envelopes, so the decryption you see below is the genuine article — there is no mock anywhere. They were not mined on a chain. This demonstrates the cryptography and the auction arithmetic, not transaction broadcast; that is a separate claim, currently blocked on a Zallet/zebra state-format gap.
